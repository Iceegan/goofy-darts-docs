# Goofy Darts Support

Need help, found a bug, or have a question about your data? Email
**[support@iceegan.com](mailto:support@iceegan.com)**. Please include your
device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account to play?**
No. Cricket and X01 both work without signing in. An account is only needed to
record your games, appear on the leaderboard, and invite an opponent by email.

**I didn't get my sign-in or confirmation email.**
Check your spam folder, then request it again from the sign-in screen. If it
still doesn't arrive, email us from the address you signed up with.

**How do I change my display name or password?**
Open **Settings**, tap your name, and edit your display name or choose
**Change Password**.

**How do I delete my account?**
Open **Settings**, tap your name, and choose **Delete Account**. Your account,
display name, and recorded throws are deleted immediately and permanently. If
you can't sign in, email us from the account's address and we will delete it
for you.

**Someone used my email address to invite me to a game and I don't want an
account.**
Email us from that address and we will delete it, or sign in with the emailed
code and use **Delete Account**.

See also the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
