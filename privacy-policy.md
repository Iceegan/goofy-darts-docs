# Goofy Darts Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 9, 2026**

Goofy Darts is a darts scoring app. This policy explains what information the
app collects, why, and how you can delete it. Questions:
[support@iceegan.com](mailto:support@iceegan.com).

## The short version

- You can play without an account. If you do, nothing you enter leaves your
  device.
- If you create an account, we store your email address, a display name, and
  the games you record, so that we can show your history and the leaderboard.
- We do not show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, or sell or share your
  information for advertising.
- You can delete your account and data at any time from inside the app.

## Playing without an account

Player names and settings you enter as a guest are stored only on your device.
Guest games are not uploaded.

## Information we collect when you have an account

| Information | Why we collect it |
|---|---|
| **Email address** | To sign you in, to send sign-in codes, account confirmation and password-reset emails. |
| **Password** | To sign you in. Stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider; we cannot read it. |
| **Display name** | Shown on the scoreboard and on the leaderboard. |
| **Account ID** | A random identifier that links your games to your account. |
| **Games you record** | Game type and settings, start and end times, who won and lost, and each dart thrown (target, multiplier, round, and time). Used to show your history and calculate leaderboard statistics. |

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or
advertising identifiers, and the app contains no advertising or analytics
software.

## Inviting an opponent

If you invite an opponent to a recorded game, you enter their email address.
We use it to email them a sign-in code, and an account is created for that
address so their side of the game can be recorded. Please only invite people
who expect it.

If you received an invitation you did not want, you can sign in with the
emailed code and delete the account, or email
[support@iceegan.com](mailto:support@iceegan.com) from that address and we
will delete it.

## Who can see your information

- **Other signed-in players** can see your display name and your leaderboard
  statistics (wins, losses, and marks per round). People who are not signed in
  cannot see the leaderboard.
- **Your email address** is never shown to other players.
- **Service providers** process data on our behalf so the app can work:
  - **Supabase** hosts the database and handles sign-in.
  - **Resend** delivers the emails described above.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else
except where the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account information and recorded games until you delete your
account.

## Deleting your account and data

In the app, open **Settings**, tap your name, and choose **Delete Account**.
Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes:

- your account, email address, and password,
- your display name, and
- every throw you recorded.

Games you played against another account holder remain in *their* history, but
no longer contain your name or any link to you. Games in which you were the
only account holder are deleted entirely.

If you cannot sign in, email
[support@iceegan.com](mailto:support@iceegan.com) from the account's address
and we will delete it for you. Deleted data may remain in our providers'
routine backups for a short time before those backups expire.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and game data and the
leaderboard are available only to signed-in users. No method of storage or transmission
is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children

Goofy Darts is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an
account, email us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
